## Decryption

The port runs the original ARMv6 Mach-O executable. App Store IPAs are
encrypted (`cryptid != 0`) and will not work. Use a decrypted copy that you
are legally entitled to use. This repository does not provide tools or
instructions for circumventing DRM.
